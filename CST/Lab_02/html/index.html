College Terminology

Syllabus
An outline of the subjects in a course of study or teaching program.
Credit Hour
A unit that measures the amount of effort required for a specific course.
GPA
Grade Point Average, a standard value used to calculate a student's average academic score.
Prerequisite
A course or requirement that must be completed before enrolling in a higher-level course.
Elective
An optional course chosen by a student according to their personal interests or career path.
