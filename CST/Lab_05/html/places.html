Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

An iconic ivory-white marble mausoleum on the right bank of the river Yamuna, built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan.

2. Amber Fort, Jaipur

A majestic hilltop fort known for its artistic Hindu style elements and breathtaking scenic views of Maota Lake.

3. Backwaters of Kerala

A serene network of interconnected canals, rivers, lakes, and inlets best explored on traditional houseboats.

4. Varanasi Ghats

One of the oldest continually inhabited cities in the world, famous for its spiritual riverfront steps along the Ganges.

5. Gateway of India, Mumbai

An arch-monument built during the 20th century in Mumbai, overlooking the Arabian Sea.
